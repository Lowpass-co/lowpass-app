## Their budget builder — categories (Annual / This Leg / Custom views)

Columns per category: CONTRACTED · SETTLED, each row carries an AUTO/Manual
provenance chip; per-show and line-item expansions.

INCOME: Show Guarantees (auto, per-show, itemized) · Merch Projections
(per-show + line items) · Production Reimbursements · Sponsorship/Other

FIXED COSTS: Bus/Ground (auto) · Trucking · Flights (auto) · Insurance ·
Rehearsal · Production · Backline

VARIABLE COSTS: Per Diem (auto) · Catering ("waterfall") · Hotels ·
Hospitality/Rider (per-show) · Merch Costs · Crew Wages (auto) · Misc/Other

OTHER (percentage-based): Commissions (pct) · Withholding Tax (pct) ·
Contingency (pct)

Extras seen: "Seed from actuals" · "Quick spreadsheet view" · eagle-eye view.
